# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This document governs participation in **this repository's spaces** — issues, pull requests, and discussions. It does not govern use of the contracts (which are permissionless and carry no terms), and it is distinct from the hosted site's rules: [foreverlibrary.xyz](https://foreverlibrary.xyz) has its own Terms of Service & Community Standards, which apply to that site and not to this repository or the protocol.

## Standards

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, especially across language and experience differences.
- Keep discussions on-topic and free of harassment, hate speech, personal attacks, and spam.
- Respect the disclosure process: security findings go to the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never to public issues first.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in edited or removed content, locked threads, or a ban from the repository's spaces, at the maintainers' discretion. Report conduct issues privately to **deployer@foreverlibrary.xyz**.
